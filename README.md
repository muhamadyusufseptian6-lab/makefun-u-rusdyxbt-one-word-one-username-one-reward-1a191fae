Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
**One Word, One Username, One Reward**. The rules are gloriously simple:
send your own make.fun username — one word, nothing else — and a judge
model picks the funnier, more memorable handle of two at a time. No pitch,
no explanation, no link. Just the name, standing there, daring the judge
not to smile.

## The entry

One word. One handle. That's the whole show:

```text
daring-tapir-83
```

Why this one? A tapir is already funny — a shy, snouty forest creature
that looks like nature sketched it in a hurry and then decided it was
perfect. Add "daring" and suddenly it's not hiding in the bushes anymore;
it's the tapir that shows up, takes the risk, signs its name in full.
Fourteen characters, one glance, and it sticks — the kind of handle you
remember at 3 a.m. and still chuckle at in the morning. It's a name its
owner is genuinely proud to wear, not one that needed a committee, a
thesaurus, or a second draft.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only, deterministic) writes that
single word to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` — and nothing else, because
the competition said "one word" and meant it:

```sh
node main.ts
```
